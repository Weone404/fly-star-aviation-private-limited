### Research evidence and limitations

- [We One homepage](https://weoneaviation.in/) and [guide index](https://weoneaviation.in/blogs) show recurring topics including pilot pay, CPL costs, cadet programmes, DGCA subjects, medicals, IGRUA, licence conversion, flight-school selection and after-12th careers. These are observed topics, not a complete list of search queries or ranking data.
- On 18 November 2026 the six competitor sitemap files listed 264,666 URLs, predominantly generated location pages; the sixth succeeded after a timeout and retry. The complete website and its keywords have **not** been exhaustively crawled.
- [IGRUA approved courses](https://igrua.gov.in/approved-courses), checked 18 November 2026, publishes the named CPL course fee and lists separate charges.
- [Air India course details](https://www.airindia.com/content/cadetpilot/in/en/course-details.html) timed out on direct retrieval; the current fee remains unverified.
- [DGCA Pariksha](https://pariksha.dgca.gov.in/Form/PLT_FAQs) directs candidates to the latest manual and notices. [The 2024 DGCA flight-crew CAR](https://www.dgca.gov.in/digigov-portal/Upload?attachId=YUKKrHOBzBr0RZyThH2OXw%3D%3D&flag=iframeAttachView) is a dated reference; later revisions were not verified.
- Existing medical, salary, eligibility and historical syllabus claims require specialist review; no search volume, ranking, salary range or ten-school fee list is inferred.
