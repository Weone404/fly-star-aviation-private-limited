Re-weighted for a **ground-training institute**, per the owner's rule:
"flying school" and FTO-implying queries excluded; ground-classes, per-subject
exam, and neutral-advisor topics promoted. Nothing here claims flight training,
a fleet, or FTO status.

## What the research changed
1. **The exam-guide cluster is the right bet and it is winnable.** vfti.co.in
   ranks with exactly the "[Exam] [YEAR]: Eligibility, Syllabus, Fees" formula.
   That formula is *more* available to a ground school than to an FTO — we
   teach these exams.
2. **Freshness gaps are the cheapest wins.** Two competitors rank on the money
   queries with visibly stale slugs: `airshipaviation.com/dgca-exam-date-2025-calendar…`
   and `goldenepaulettes.com/how-to-convert-foreign-cpl-to-indian-license` dated
   2025. Annual-refresh topics are structurally winnable.
3. **Golden Epaulettes is competitor #1 across the board** — it ranked in 5 of
   8 baseline queries and again for conversion and cadet topics. Assume it
   contests every topic below.
4. **One genuinely under-owned topic surfaced: DGCA OLODE** (Online On-Demand
   Examination). It is current, it is confusing to candidates, and no ranking
   page explains it well.
5. **The neutral-advisor angle is the moat.** "How to choose ground classes",
   "ground classes vs self-study", "cadet vs self-sponsored" — a ground school
   can write these credibly; an FTO selling flying hours cannot. Answer engines
   cite advisors over sellers.

## ⚠️ Fact conflict found — do not publish either figure yet
The W8 baseline logged "DGCA exams in January, April, July, October" from a
secondary source. This session's research says Regular Flight Crew sessions run
**March, June, September, December**, plus on-demand OLODE windows. **Two
secondary sources disagree.** The only acceptable source is the official
programme of examinations on `pariksha.dgca.gov.in`. Marked `[CONFIRM]` in the
log; any article touching exam dates must cite the DGCA PDF directly.
