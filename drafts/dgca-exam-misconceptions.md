Much of the advice circulating about DGCA Flight Crew examinations is out of
date or simply wrong — on qualifications, age limits, and how the Computer
Number works. As of August 2026, DGCA's own published FAQ on the Pariksha
portal settles most of these directly. Below is what the authority actually
states, alongside the belief it contradicts, with the questions that genuinely
remain open kept separate.

## Quick reference

| Widely believed | What DGCA states (as of August 2026) |
|---|---|
| You need PCM — Physics, Chemistry, Maths | 10+2 with **Physics and Mathematics** |
| There is an upper age limit to register | **No maximum age limit** |
| The Computer Number expires | Validity is **lifetime** |
| You can hold more than one | **One per candidate** |
| Submitting online is the whole application | NEW candidates must also **post a hard copy** |
| Indian candidates need a passport | **Not required** for Indian candidates |
| A rejected form gets your money back | Fee is **never refundable** |

## Does DGCA require Chemistry? {#pcm}

**Misconception:** DGCA requires PCM — Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics.

**What DGCA states:** applicants, except in the PPL category, must have passed
10+2 with **Physics and Mathematics** from a recognised board or university, or
its equivalent. Chemistry does not appear in the stated requirement.

This one matters because it changes who believes they are eligible. A candidate
who took Physics and Mathematics without Chemistry may have been told, wrongly,
that they cannot proceed.

Two related requirements are real and often missed. If your 10th or 10+2 came
from an international school, board or university — whether in India or abroad
— you need an equivalency certificate from the Association of Indian
Universities. Diploma holders need a 10+2 equivalency certificate from the
relevant state Directorate of Technical Education or a recognised institution.

## Is there an age limit? {#age}

**Misconception:** you must be under a certain age to register for DGCA exams.

**What DGCA states:** there is **no maximum age limit** to register as a Flight
Crew candidate.

The confusion is understandable, because age ceilings do exist elsewhere in the
industry — airline cadet programmes and direct-entry recruitment set their own
limits, and those are real. But they are the airline's rules, not DGCA's, and
they apply at a different stage. Registering for and passing DGCA theory papers
is not age-capped.

## Does the Computer Number expire? {#validity}

**Misconception:** the Computer Number lapses and has to be renewed.

**What DGCA states:** its validity is **lifetime**.

A single number covers every Flight Crew examination category DGCA conducts.
Moving between categories means updating your profile's qualification and
licence details, not applying again.

## Can you hold more than one Computer Number? {#one}

**Misconception:** you can hold separate numbers for different examinations.

**What DGCA states:** a candidate is authorised to hold **only one**.

If you believe you may already have been allotted one — for instance, before
the Pariksha portal launched — you are what DGCA calls an OLD candidate, and
you register rather than reapply. Applying afresh when you already hold a
number is a route to rejection.

## Is submitting online enough? {#hardcopy}

**Misconception:** completing the online application is the whole process.

**What DGCA states:** for NEW candidates it is not. After Final Submission you
must print the PDF form emailed to you, affix the same photograph you uploaded,
attach all supporting documents in the printed sequence, and send it by Speed
Post or Registered Post to the Central Examination Organization in New Delhi.
The number is allotted only after DGCA scrutinises the online application
**against** that hard copy.

OLD candidates do not need to send hard copies.

## Do Indian candidates need a passport? {#passport}

**Misconception:** a passport is required to register.

**What DGCA states:** a passport is **not mandatory for Indian candidates**. It
is mandatory for foreign candidates — whose passport address becomes their
permanent address on record — and for candidates from Nepal and Bhutan. Foreign
nationals additionally require an Indian mobile number before registering, and
must undergo security clearance.

## Are exam fees refundable? {#refund}

**Misconception:** a rejected examination form means your fee comes back.

**What DGCA states:** "The fee paid once for examination is NOT REFUNDABLE
under any circumstances."

DGCA's published fee for the Flight Crew Licence online examination is ₹2,500
per paper. Combined with the non-refundability, the practical consequence is
simple: confirm your eligibility and your documents *before* you pay, because a
rejection is not recoverable.

## Document format traps that get applications rejected {#formats}

These are not misconceptions so much as details people miss, and each one is a
rejection waiting to happen.

| Requirement | What DGCA states |
|---|---|
| Documents | **PDF only.** JPEG is not accepted for documents |
| Photograph and signature | **JPEG/JPG only.** PDF is not accepted for these |
| Photograph | 45 mm × 35 mm, white background, no border, face ≈70% of frame, matt finish, ≤70 KB, not more than 3 months old |
| Signature | 20 mm × 45 mm, white background, no border, ≤20 KB |
| After Final Submission | **Nothing further can be uploaded** |
| Registration email link | Valid **24 hours**; if it expires you must register again |
| Board not in the dropdown | Select **"OTHERS"** and proceed |
| Board Verification Certificate | **Mandatory for all NEW candidates** |

## Still unsettled {#unsettled}

Not everything has a clean answer, and it is more useful to say so than to
guess.

**Whether Computer Numbers are now auto-generated.** Press coverage has
described DGCA introducing automatic Computer Number generation for flight crew
candidates. As of August 2026, DGCA's own published FAQ states the number is
allotted only after scrutiny of the online application against the posted hard
copy — that is, not automatically. Either the FAQ has not caught up with a
rollout, or the coverage overstates it. **Check the Pariksha portal's current
notices and user manual before you plan around either version.**

**Which months carry regular examination sessions.** Widely circulated
schedules disagree with each other. The DGCA examination calendar published on
the Pariksha portal is the only authority, and it is reissued annually. Do not
plan around a month you read on a third-party page.

**The on-demand examination fee.** DGCA's published FAQ states ₹2,500 per paper
for the Flight Crew Licence online examination and does not give a separate
figure for the on-demand format. Figures circulating for OLODE are not
confirmed by that source.

## FAQ {#faq}

**Does DGCA require Chemistry at 10+2?**
No. Except for the PPL category, DGCA states the requirement as 10+2 with
Physics and Mathematics from a recognised board or university, or its
equivalent.

**Is there a maximum age to register for DGCA exams?**
No. DGCA states there is no maximum age limit to register as a Flight Crew
candidate. Age limits that exist in cadet programmes and airline recruitment
are set by the airline, not by DGCA.

**Does the DGCA Computer Number expire?**
No. Its validity is lifetime, and one number covers every Flight Crew
examination category.

**Can I hold two Computer Numbers?**
No. A candidate is authorised to hold only one.

**Is the online application the whole process?**
Not for NEW candidates. The application is allotted only after DGCA scrutinises
the online submission against a hard copy sent by Speed Post or Registered Post
to the Central Examination Organization in New Delhi.

**Do I need a passport to register as an Indian candidate?**
No. A passport is mandatory for foreign candidates, and for candidates from
Nepal and Bhutan, but not for Indian candidates.

**Will I get my examination fee back if my form is rejected?**
No. DGCA states the fee paid once is not refundable under any circumstances.

**What format do documents have to be in?**
Documents must be PDF. The photograph and signature must be JPEG or JPG.

**Can I upload a document I forgot, after submitting?**
No. Nothing can be uploaded after Final Submission.

**What if my school board is not in the dropdown list?**
DGCA advises selecting "OTHERS" and proceeding with registration.
