DGCA runs its Flight Crew Licence papers in two formats. Regular sessions are
fixed-date exams held on a published calendar. OLODE — the Online On-Demand
Examination — lets you pick your own date and slot from whatever the Pariksha
portal has open, at a higher fee per paper. Both are conducted by DGCA's Central
Examination Organization under Rule 41A of the Aircraft Rules, 1937, and a pass
in either counts identically toward your licence.

## What is OLODE? {#what-is-olode}

OLODE stands for Online On-Demand Examination. It is a DGCA exam format in which
the candidate selects an examination date and time from slots published on the
Pariksha portal, rather than waiting for a fixed session date. Seats per slot are
limited and allotted first come, first served. The paper, the syllabus and the
resulting pass are identical to a regular session.

The word "on-demand" describes scheduling, not difficulty or content. An OLODE
Air Navigation paper is the same examination as an Air Navigation paper written
in a regular session — same conducting authority, same syllabus, same weight
toward your Commercial Pilot Licence.

DGCA publishes separate available-seat listings for Flight Crew and for Aircraft
Maintenance Engineers, so the AME and FC schedules are managed independently.

## How is OLODE different from a regular session? {#difference}

The difference is when you sit, not what you sit. Regular sessions run on a
published annual calendar with fixed dates for each paper. OLODE lets you book a
slot when it suits you, subject to seat availability at your chosen centre. The
trade is scheduling control against a higher per-paper fee.

| | Regular session | OLODE |
|---|---|---|
| **Date** | Fixed, published in the annual calendar | Chosen by you from open slots |
| **Fee per paper** | ₹2,500 | [CONFIRM: reported ₹5,000] |
| **Seats** | Session capacity | Limited per slot, first come first served |
| **Centres** | Published per session | New Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai, Kolkata, Bengaluru |
| **Syllabus** | Identical | Identical |
| **Value of a pass** | Identical | Identical |
| **Best for** | Several papers at once, budget-led planning | Re-attempting one paper, or a hard personal deadline |

## What does each format cost? {#cost}

A regular Flight Crew Licence paper costs ₹2,500, stated directly in DGCA's own
Pariksha FAQ. OLODE is widely reported to cost double that per paper, though
DGCA's published FAQ does not state an OLODE-specific figure. Payment runs
through Bharatkosh by net banking, debit or credit card, and no fee is
refundable under any circumstance.

That last point deserves weight. If your application is rejected — wrong
documents, an ineligible profile, a missed deadline — the money is gone. Check
your eligibility and your uploads before you pay, not after.

Across a full CPL theory load the difference compounds. Four papers at the
regular fee is ₹10,000; the same four at the reported OLODE rate is ₹20,000.
For most candidates that argues for regular sessions as the default and OLODE as
the exception, used deliberately.

## Where are OLODE exams held? {#centres}

OLODE examinations are conducted at designated centres in New Delhi, Mumbai,
Chennai, Kolkata and Bengaluru. Candidates outside those cities should budget
travel and an overnight stay into the real cost of an on-demand attempt.

Report at least 15 minutes before your scheduled time. Entry closes 30 minutes
before the examination begins — arriving late means forfeiting a non-refundable
fee.

## How do you apply for an OLODE slot? {#apply}

You apply through the Pariksha portal with an allotted Computer Number, choose
an available slot, and pay through Bharatkosh. The DGCA user manual on the
portal carries the current step-by-step instructions, which change from time to
time.

1. Log in at pariksha.dgca.gov.in with your candidate credentials. A Computer
   Number is required first — it is a one-per-candidate, lifetime identifier
   allotted by the CEO.
2. Open the Flight Crew OLODE available-seats listing and identify an open slot
   at a centre you can reach.
3. Select the paper and slot, and complete the application form.
4. Pay through Bharatkosh. The payment is final.
5. Download the admit card and confirm the reporting time.
6. Reach the centre at least 15 minutes early, remembering entry closes 30
   minutes before the start.

## Which format should you choose? {#choose}

Choose regular sessions when you are writing several papers and cost matters,
which describes most CPL candidates. Choose OLODE when timing genuinely
outweighs money: a single re-attempt blocking your licence issue, a training
slot abroad you must not miss, or an airline application window closing before
the next regular session.

Two honest cautions. First, on-demand scheduling only helps if you are actually
prepared — booking an earlier date does not create readiness, and the fee is
non-refundable either way. Second, seats are finite and first come, first
served, so an OLODE plan is not guaranteed until the slot is confirmed.

## What does OLODE not change? {#unchanged}

OLODE changes scheduling and fee. It does not change eligibility, syllabus,
paper validity, or anything about your Computer Number. A pass carries the same
weight, your Computer Number stays the same, and the syllabus is unchanged.

## Common misconceptions {#misconceptions}

Several requirements around DGCA Flight Crew examinations are widely
misreported. Each correction below is DGCA's own published text.

**Misconception: DGCA requires PCM — Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics.**
The actual requirement is **10+2 with Physics and Mathematics** from a
recognised board or university, or its equivalent, per DGCA's Pariksha FAQ.
Chemistry is not part of the stated requirement. The PPL category is excepted.

**Misconception: there is an upper age limit for registering.**
DGCA states there is **no maximum age limit** to register as a Flight Crew
candidate. Age ceilings you may have read about belong to airline recruitment
and cadet programmes, which are separate from DGCA registration.

**Misconception: you can hold more than one Computer Number.**
A candidate is authorised to hold **only one**, and its validity is lifetime.
One number covers every Flight Crew examination category.

**Misconception: a rejected exam form gets your money back.**
DGCA states the fee paid once is **not refundable under any circumstances**,
including rejection. Verify eligibility and documents before paying.

**Misconception: OLODE is an easier or separate exam.**
It is the same paper under the same syllabus and the same rule. Only the
scheduling and the fee differ.

## FAQ {#faq}

**Is an OLODE pass worth the same as a regular session pass?**
Yes. Both are Flight Crew Licence examinations conducted by DGCA's Central
Examination Organization under Rule 41A of the Aircraft Rules, 1937. The format
affects when you sit the paper, not what the pass is worth.

**How much does a DGCA Flight Crew paper cost?**
DGCA's Pariksha FAQ states ₹2,500 per paper for the Flight Crew Licence online
examination. [CONFIRM: OLODE is widely reported at ₹5,000 per paper, but DGCA's
published FAQ does not state a separate OLODE figure.]

**Can I get a refund if my exam form is rejected?**
No. DGCA states the fee paid once is not refundable under any circumstances.
Verify your eligibility and documents before paying.

**Where are OLODE exams conducted?**
At designated centres in New Delhi, Mumbai, Chennai, Kolkata and Bengaluru.

**Do I need a Computer Number before booking OLODE?**
Yes. The Computer Number is a unique lifetime identifier allotted by the CEO
after your application is approved, and it is required for every DGCA Flight
Crew examination.

**Can I hold more than one Computer Number?**
No. DGCA authorises only one Computer Number per candidate.

**How early should I reach the exam centre?**
At least 15 minutes before your scheduled time. Entry closes 30 minutes before
the examination starts.

**Is OLODE available for AME candidates too?**
Yes. DGCA publishes separate available-seat listings for Aircraft Maintenance
Engineers and for Flight Crew.

**Which subjects can I write through OLODE?**
Flight Crew papers offered in the on-demand format at the time of booking.
Check the current available-seats listing on the portal, since availability
varies by session and centre.

**Does OLODE improve my chances of passing?**
No. It changes only your exam date. Preparation determines the outcome, and the
fee is non-refundable whichever format you choose.
