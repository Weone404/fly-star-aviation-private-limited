## ⚠️ Do not cite this document as current law

Two independent problems, either one disqualifying:

1. **It is stale.** The latest amendment visible in the text is **GSR No. 11(E)
   dated 10-01-2011**. Nothing later appears. That is fifteen years of possible
   amendment unaccounted for.
2. **The OCR is bad.** The scan renders "1937" as "1997", "39C" as "29C",
   "validity" as "vality", "medical fitness" as "mecical finess", and — the one
   that matters most — the item references in sub-rule (5) as "items () and (i)".

It is banked because it establishes **what Rule 39C is about and roughly what it
says**, which is worth having. It is not banked as a citable source.

## What this does and does not settle

**Shape of the answer, now known.** Medical validity and licence validity are set
by **Rule 39C of the Aircraft Rules — not by a CAR.** `BROWSER-FETCH.md` item 3
was aimed at the wrong document for the validity question.

**Not publishable from this copy:**

- **Any figure in the table.** They are legible and mutually consistent, but this
  copy is fifteen years old and the site publishes no other date.
- **The age-forty halving.** This is the most-searched part of the rule — every
  "Class 1 medical validity after 40" query lands on it — and it is the one clause
  the OCR destroyed. "items () and (i)" cannot be read. The obvious reading is the
  two twelve-month categories, and *the obvious reading is not a source.*

**What would settle it:** Rule 39C from a maintained copy — India Code's, or the
DGCA portal page for Rule 39C. Two things to capture: **which table items
sub-rule (5) names**, and **the amendment the copy is current to**, so it can be
compared against GSR 11(E) of 10-01-2011 above.
