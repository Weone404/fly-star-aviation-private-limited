## Corrections this source forces on widely-repeated claims
- **Qualification is 10+2 with Physics and Mathematics** (FAQ 7) — NOT "PCM".
  Several ranking competitor pages state Physics, Chemistry AND Mathematics.
  We must not repeat that error.
- **There is no maximum age limit** to register as a Flight Crew candidate
  (FAQ 8). "Pilot age limit" content elsewhere conflates DGCA registration
  with airline recruitment ceilings — these are different things.
- **Computer Number validity is lifetime** and a candidate may hold **only
  one** (FAQ 2, 3).
- **Exam fee is Rs. 2,500 per paper and is never refundable** (FAQ 13, 14).
- **Legal basis: Rule 41A of Aircraft Rules 1937**; educational qualification
  under **Rule 47A / Schedule II** (FAQ 12, 18).
- Exams are conducted by the **Central Examination Organization (CEO)**,
  East Block III, Level III, R.K. Puram, New Delhi 110066 (FAQ 15).

FAQ No. 1. What is the Computer Number? Computer Number is Unique Id allotted to a candidate after his application of Computer Number is approved by CEO, DGCA. 

FAQ No. 2. How many computer number can a Candidate have? A Candidate is authorised to have only one computer number. 

FAQ No. 3. What is the validity of computer number? Computer number validity is life time. 

FAQ No. 4. Is computer number issued by DGCA is applicable on all category of Flight Crew Examination? Yes there will be only one computer number for all flight crew examination of DGCA. 

FAQ No. 5. Is the same computer can be used for FATA, FDEG etc? Yes but candidate has to update his profile Qualification/Licence details accordingly. 

FAQ No. 6. What is the name of portal to register for Flight Crew? The name of portal to register for Flight Crew is “pariksha.dgca.gov.in”. 

FAQ No. 7. What is the minimum qualification required to register as a Flight Crew Candidate in pariksha.dgca.gov.in? The applicant except PPL category must have passed 10+2 examination with Physics and Mathematics from a recognized board or university or its equivalent, for details refer user manual available on www.dgca.gov.in and pariksha.dgca.gov.in. 

FAQ No. 8. What is the maximum age to register as a Flight Crew Candidate in pariksha.dgca.gov.in? There is no maximum age limit to register as an Flight Crew Candidate in pariksha.dgca.gov.in. 

FAQ No. 9. Where are the requirements related to Flight Crew Licence Examinations available? Requirements related to Flight Crew Livcense examination are in respective CAR Section 7, series B-part I, G-Part II, H, Section 2, L-Pat XV and M and available on www.dgca.nic.in website. 

FAQ No. 10. Which examinations are conducted under Flight Crew? FATA,FDEG,FE, FN & Pilot. 

FAQ No. 11. Where I can see the rules related to Flight Crew? The rules related to Flight Crew License examin ations are in Aircraft rule 1937 available in www.dgca.nic.in website. 

FAQ No. 12. DGCA conduct Flight Crew Licence examination according to which rule? DGCA conducts of Flight Crew Licence Examination under Rule of 41A of Aircraft Rules 1937. 

FAQ No. 13. What is fee for Flight Crew Online Examination of DGCA? Fee for Flight Crew Licence online examination is Rs. 2500/- per paper 

FAQ No. 14. Is the fee paid once can be refundable if examination form is rejected? The fee paid once for examination is NOT REFUNDABLE under any circumstances. 

FAQ No. 15. Which office conducts examination of Flight Crew Licence? The Central Examination Organization (CEO) O/o DGCA, located at East Block III, Level III, R.K. Puram, New Delhi 110066 is responsible for conducting Flight Crew Licence Examinations. 

FAQ No. 16. Who are called NEW Candidates? Candidate not having any Flight Crew computer number till date from CEO. 

FAQ No. 17. Who are called OLD Candidates? All candidates, who have been allotted computer number prior to launch of this portal are OLD candidates. 

FAQ No. 18. What is Aircraft Rule 47A? Aircraft Rule 47A defines Minimum educational qualification for holding a license- No person shall be granted a license unless he possesses the educational qualification laid down in Schedule II: Provided that candidates already enrolled for training on or before the coming into force of these rules with flying club or institution shall be exempted from application of this rule. 

FAQ No. 19. Is it required to send Hard Copy of Computer Number Allotment Applications of NEW Candidates? Yes, candidate needs to take a print of pdf form sent in her/his registered email ID and paste recent passport size photograph (as uploaded) and attach all relevant uploaded documents (Attested by / Self attested as applicable) as per list in printed form, in same sequence and send it to CEO O/o DGCA, located at East Block III, Level III, R.K. Puram, New Delhi 110066. 

FAQ No. 20. What is the address at which the Computer Number Allotment Applications (HARD Copy) of NEW Candidates to be sent? The Computer Number Allotment Applications (HARD Copy) of NEW Candidates shall be send through Speed Post/ Registered post to “Central Examination Organization (CEO) O/o Director General of Civil Aviation, East Block-III, Level-III, R.K. Puram, New Delhi – 110066”. 

FAQ No. 21. Is it required to send Hard Copy of Registration Form to CEO of OLD Candidates? No, OLD Candidates need not to send hard copies of the documents. 

FAQ No. 22. How to track the Computer Number Allotment Applications of NEW Candidates? Computer Number Allotment Applications of NEW Candidates (having Temp. Id.) may be track through “Candidate Login” using credentials in pariksha.dgca.gov.in 

FAQ No. 23. How to raise any query to CEO? Candidate can raise any query through 'Help Desk' tab. 

FAQ No. 24. How to check the details of previous pass papers for OLD candidates? Examination History of registered candidate can be checked through “Candidate Login” using her/his credentials on pariksha.dgca.gov.in. 

FAQ No. 25. Who requires equivalency certificate from Association of Indian Universities? Those Applicants who have acquired the Basic Qualification (10th, 10+2 or equivalent) from other than Council of Boards of Secondary Education, State/Central Govt. Education Board i.e. any International School / Board / University situated in India or abroad . 

FAQ No. 26. Where AIU is situated? Association of Indian Universities (AIU) House, 16, Kotla Marg, New Delhi – 110022 

FAQ No. 27. Do Diploma holder requires 10+2 Equivalent certificate? Yes, Diploma holders must have 10+2 Equivalent certificate from respective Directorate of Technical Education of state govt./ Institutions/ University situated in India or abroad recognised by Indian Boards/ Institutions. 

FAQ No. 28. Who requires Security clearance? All Foreign National candidates have to undergo Security clearance process while applying for Allotment of Computer Number, for this She/ He has to fill up Security clearance Form (Annexure A) in quintuplicate and send it “Central Examination Organization (CEO) O/o Director General of Civil Aviation, East Block-III, Level-III, R.K. Puram, New Delhi – 110066” along with computer number application form. 

FAQ No. 29. Is it mandatory to have passport for Indian candidates? No, Passport is not mandatory for Indian candidates. 

FAQ No. 30. Passport is mandatory for whom? All Foreign candidates require valid Passport before applying for Allotment of Computer Number as address in passport will be used as permanent address of candidate. 

FAQ No. 31. Is the Indian mobile number is mandatory for foreign candidates before registration in pariksha.dgca.gov.in? Yes, Indian mobile number is mandatory for foreign candidates before registration in pariksha.dgca.gov.in 

FAQ No. 32. Is the passport is mandatory for Nepal & Bhutan Candidates? Yes, Nepal & Bhutan Candidates should have a valid passport. 

FAQ No. 33. What is Board Verification Certificates (BVC)? Board Verification Certificate is a certificate provided by respective boards to certify the authenticity of Mark sheet of 10th /10+2/10+2 equivalent/Diploma. 

FAQ No. 34. For which education qualification Board Verification Certificates is required? It is required for the All Indian & foreign candidate’s education Mark sheet of 10th /10+2/ /Diploma/10+2 equivalent 

FAQ No. 35. Is the Board Verification Certificates mandatory for all NEW candidates before registration in pariksha.dgca.gov.in? Yes it is mandatory for all new candidates. 

FAQ No. 36. To whom the Board Verification Certificates is not mandatory before registration in pariksha.dgca.gov.in? OLD candidates do not required the Board Verification Certificates. 

FAQ No. 37. What is the specification required to upload photo of candidates? The Size of the colour photograph has to be 45mm height X 35mm width taken on a white background without borders. The face on the photograph should be approximately 70% of the Photograph. The photograph should be of MATT finish. The size of the scanned colour photo image should not be more than 70kb (not more than 3 months old). 

FAQ No. 38. What is the specification required to upload signature of candidates? The size of the scanned signature image is 20mm height X 45mm width on a white background without border. The size of the scanned Signature image should not be more than 20kb. 

FAQ No. 39. Can I upload photo & signature in pdf Format? No, the photograph and Signature must be in JPEG/JPG Format only. 

FAQ No. 40. Can I upload documents in Jpeg Format? No, all documents must be in pdf Format only. 

FAQ No. 41. What is the maximum size of documents can be uploaded in online portal? For size of document, refer the document size table in User Manual. 

FAQ No. 42. If I forget any document to upload, can I upload it latter on after “Final Submission” of the form? No, after Final Submit, it is not possible. 

FAQ No. 43. After submitting the Registration Form, link on the registered email address can be activated upto how long? After submitting of the Registration Form, candidate will receive link on the registered email address. Link valid till 24 hours only. 

FAQ No. 44. If Link is not activated within 24 hours, then how the candidate will proceed further? Candidate needs to re-register again. 

FAQ No. 45. If name of School Board/Institute is not available in drop down, then how to complete the Computer Number Allotment Applications? If name of the school board/ institute is not available in dropdown list, candidates are advised to select “OTHERS” and proceed for Registration. 

FAQ No. 46. After final submission of computer number allotment applications will the candidate get the Computer number automatically generated by online system? No, Computer Number will be allotted after scrutiny and approval of online submitted application vis-a-vis hard copy of the same application sent by candidate to CEO, DGCA. 

FAQ No. 47. If Computer Number Application is rejected, then how will I come to know the reason of rejection? After rejection the candidate will receive the email at her/his registered email id with the reason of rejection. 

FAQ No. 48. If Computer Number Applications is approved, then how wouId I come to know the allotted computer number? After approval of the Computer Number Application, candidate would receive the email at her/his registered email id with the allotted Computer Number. 

FAQ No. 49. After Allotment of computer number, what will be the candidate login Id? After Allotment of computer number, candidate login Id wil be her/his allotted computer number including prefix “P-”. 

FAQ No. 50. After rejection of Computer Number Application, what is the procedure to apply again? After rejection of Computer Number Application, candidate need to login through “Candidate Login” using their credentials i.e her/his email id register with us and correct/comply all rejections listed in rejection mail. 

FAQ No. 51. When can a NEW candidate update his profile? NEW candidate can update his profile only after allotment of computer number. 

FAQ No. 52. If the saved data of OLD candidate found mismatch during registration, then should I continue the registration & submit the form? Yes, registration shall be completed with save data only, after that candidate can modify the mismatch data with the help of profile Management link by login credentials. 

FAQ No. 53. Which profile data can be updated by candidates? Candidate can update the mobile number, email id and correspondence address using profile management link, which do not required approval from CEO, DGCA. 

FAQ No. 54. Which profile data is required approval from CEO, DGCA ? ALL the data other than mobile number, email id and correspondence address are required prior approval from CEO, DGCA. 

FAQ No. 55. What is the procedure for request to update the profile, which need CEO, DGCA approval? Raise query tab is available in profile management which can be use to put the request for update profile after login using her/his credentials. 

FAQ No. 56. How to change the password ? To change password, login using her/his credentials and select change password tab. 

FAQ No. 57. What can i do if i forgot my password? Go on the login page and select forgot password tab use your email id register with pariksha.dgca.gov.in 

FAQ No. 58. Can I share my mobile number and email Id with others for registration? No, it is not possible to use same mobile number and email id for more than one candidate.
