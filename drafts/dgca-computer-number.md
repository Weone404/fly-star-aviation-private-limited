A DGCA Computer Number is the unique lifetime identifier allotted to a Flight
Crew candidate by the Central Examination Organization, Office of the DGCA. You
cannot apply for any DGCA pilot examination without one. A candidate may hold
only one, it covers every Flight Crew examination category, and it is issued
only after DGCA scrutinises both your online application and the hard copy you
post to Delhi.

## What is a DGCA Computer Number? {#what}

A Computer Number is the unique identity DGCA assigns to a Flight Crew
candidate, and it is the prerequisite for every DGCA pilot examination. It is
allotted by the Central Examination Organization after your application is
approved, its validity is lifetime, and a candidate is authorised to hold only
one.

The same number covers every Flight Crew examination category DGCA conducts —
Pilot, FATA, FDEG, FE and FN. If you move between categories, you update your
profile's qualification and licence details rather than applying for a new
number.

After allotment, your portal login ID becomes your Computer Number with the
prefix **"P-"**.

## Who is eligible? {#eligibility}

Except for the PPL category, applicants must have passed 10+2 with **Physics and
Mathematics** from a recognised board or university, or an equivalent. DGCA
states there is **no maximum age limit** to register as a Flight Crew candidate.

Two situations need an extra certificate:

- **Qualifications from outside the Indian board system.** Anyone whose 10th,
  10+2 or equivalent came from an international school, board or university —
  whether located in India or abroad — needs an equivalency certificate from the
  Association of Indian Universities (AIU House, 16 Kotla Marg, New Delhi
  110022).
- **Diploma holders.** A 10+2 equivalency certificate is required from the
  relevant state Directorate of Technical Education, or from a recognised
  institution or university.

Foreign nationals face three additional requirements: a valid passport (the
passport address becomes the permanent address on record), an Indian mobile
number before registering, and security clearance — Annexure A, submitted **in
quintuplicate** by post along with the application. Candidates from Nepal and
Bhutan also require a valid passport. Indian candidates do not need a passport
at all.

## NEW candidate or OLD candidate? {#new-old}

DGCA divides applicants into two groups, and the paperwork differs sharply. A
NEW candidate has never held a Flight Crew Computer Number. An OLD candidate was
allotted one before the Pariksha portal launched.

| | NEW candidate | OLD candidate |
|---|---|---|
| **Definition** | No Flight Crew Computer Number from CEO to date | Number allotted before the portal launched |
| **Hard copy by post** | Required | Not required |
| **Board Verification Certificate** | Mandatory | Not required |
| **Profile updates** | Only after the number is allotted | Via profile management after registering |

If your saved data as an OLD candidate shows a mismatch during registration,
DGCA's guidance is to complete registration with the saved data anyway, then
correct it afterwards through the profile management link.

## What documents do you need? {#documents}

NEW candidates need their educational mark sheets, a Board Verification
Certificate for each, and the supporting documents listed on the generated
application form — all uploaded as PDFs.

A **Board Verification Certificate (BVC)** is issued by the relevant board to
certify that your mark sheet is authentic. It is required for the 10th, 10+2,
10+2-equivalent or Diploma mark sheets of Indian and foreign candidates alike,
and it is **mandatory for all NEW candidates before registration**. OLD
candidates do not need one.

Two format rules that reject applications routinely:

- **All documents must be PDF.** JPEG is not accepted for documents.
- **Photograph and signature must be JPEG/JPG.** PDF is not accepted for these.

One rule deserves particular attention: **after Final Submission you cannot
upload anything further.** A forgotten document means correcting a rejection
later, not adding a file. Assemble everything before you submit.

## Photo and signature specifications {#specs}

DGCA publishes exact specifications, and mismatches are a common rejection
reason.

| | Photograph | Signature |
|---|---|---|
| **Dimensions** | 45 mm high × 35 mm wide | 20 mm high × 45 mm wide |
| **Background** | White, no border | White, no border |
| **Maximum file size** | 70 KB | 20 KB |
| **Format** | JPEG/JPG only | JPEG/JPG only |
| **Other** | Face approximately 70% of the frame; matt finish; not more than 3 months old | — |

## How to apply, step by step {#apply}

You register on the Pariksha portal, activate the emailed link within 24 hours,
complete the form, upload documents, submit, then post a printed hard copy with
attached documents to the CEO in New Delhi. The number is allotted only after
DGCA scrutinises both.

1. **Register** at pariksha.dgca.gov.in under the Flight Crew section as a new
   candidate.
2. **Activate the email link within 24 hours.** It expires after that, and an
   expired link means starting registration again from scratch.
3. **Complete the application** with personal, educational and address details.
   If your school board or institute is not in the dropdown, select **"OTHERS"**
   and continue.
4. **Upload documents** — PDFs for documents, JPEG for photo and signature,
   within the size limits above.
5. **Final Submit.** Check everything first; nothing can be added afterwards.
6. **Print the PDF form** sent to your registered email, affix the same
   passport-size photograph you uploaded, and attach all supporting documents —
   self-attested or attested as applicable — **in the same sequence as the
   printed list**.
7. **Post it by Speed Post or Registered Post** to: Central Examination
   Organization (CEO), O/o Director General of Civil Aviation, East Block-III,
   Level-III, R.K. Puram, New Delhi – 110066.
8. **Track** the application through Candidate Login using your temporary ID.

Foreign nationals additionally enclose the security clearance form (Annexure A)
in quintuplicate with the same posted application.

## What happens after you submit? {#after}

DGCA scrutinises the online application against the posted hard copy. The number
is **not** generated automatically on submission. If approved, the allotted
Computer Number arrives by email at your registered address.

Once allotted, your login ID becomes the number prefixed with "P-", and you can
update your profile for the first time. Note what you can change yourself and
what you cannot:

- **No approval needed:** mobile number, email ID, correspondence address.
- **CEO approval needed:** everything else. Raise it through the "Raise query"
  tab in profile management.

## If your application is rejected {#rejected}

Rejections are emailed to your registered address with the reason stated. You
then log in through Candidate Login, correct every item listed in the rejection
email, and resubmit.

Worth planning around: DGCA states the examination fee is not refundable under
any circumstances, so it is worth being certain of your eligibility and your
documents before money is involved at the examination stage.

## Common misconceptions {#misconceptions}

**Misconception: DGCA requires PCM — Physics, Chemistry and Mathematics.**
The stated requirement is **10+2 with Physics and Mathematics** from a
recognised board or university, or its equivalent. Chemistry is not part of it.
The PPL category is excepted.

**Misconception: there is an upper age limit to register.**
DGCA states there is **no maximum age limit** to register as a Flight Crew
candidate. Age ceilings belong to airline recruitment and cadet programmes,
which are separate from DGCA registration.

**Misconception: the Computer Number expires or needs renewal.**
Its validity is **lifetime**.

**Misconception: you can hold a second number for a different exam category.**
A candidate is authorised to hold **only one**, and it covers every Flight Crew
examination category.

**Misconception: submitting online is enough.**
For NEW candidates it is not. The number is allotted only after DGCA scrutinises
the online application **against the hard copy** posted to the CEO.

**Misconception: Indian candidates need a passport.**
They do not. A passport is mandatory for foreign candidates, including those
from Nepal and Bhutan.

## FAQ {#faq}

**What is a DGCA Computer Number?**
It is the unique identity allotted to a Flight Crew candidate by the Central
Examination Organization, Office of the DGCA, after the candidate's application
is approved. It is required to apply for any DGCA pilot examination.

**How long is a Computer Number valid?**
Its validity is lifetime.

**Can I have more than one Computer Number?**
No. A candidate is authorised to hold only one, and it applies to all Flight
Crew examination categories.

**What qualification do I need?**
Except for the PPL category, applicants must have passed 10+2 with Physics and
Mathematics from a recognised board or university, or an equivalent.

**Is there a maximum age to register?**
No. DGCA states there is no maximum age limit to register as a Flight Crew
candidate.

**Do I have to post a hard copy?**
NEW candidates do — by Speed Post or Registered Post to the CEO at East
Block-III, Level-III, R.K. Puram, New Delhi 110066. OLD candidates do not.

**Is the Computer Number generated automatically after I submit?**
No. It is allotted only after DGCA scrutinises the online application against
the hard copy posted by the candidate.

**What is a Board Verification Certificate?**
A certificate from the relevant board certifying that your 10th, 10+2,
10+2-equivalent or Diploma mark sheet is authentic. It is mandatory for all NEW
candidates.

**Can I upload documents as JPEG?**
No. Documents must be PDF. Only the photograph and signature are JPEG/JPG.

**Can I add a missing document after Final Submission?**
No. Nothing can be uploaded after Final Submit.

**How long is the registration email link valid?**
24 hours. If it is not activated in that window, you must register again.

**My school board is not in the dropdown. What do I do?**
Select "OTHERS" and proceed with registration.

**What will my login ID be after allotment?**
Your allotted Computer Number with the prefix "P-".

**Which profile details can I change myself?**
Mobile number, email ID and correspondence address. Everything else requires
prior approval from the CEO, DGCA, requested through the "Raise query" tab.
