# Content Quality Findings — flystar.co.in

## Strengths
- Wide, intent-matched coverage: CPL/ATPL/PPL, DGCA (medical, computer number, ground classes, RTR, board verification, full-form), country training (India/USA/NZ/South Africa/Australia/Maldives/Sri Lanka), airline-interview prep (Air India, IndiGo), and B2B services (MRO, CAMO, charter, aircraft management/sourcing, livery, consultancy).
- E-E-A-T anchors: founded 2008, Dwarka address, phone, external profiles.
- One H1 per page across all 42 page files.

## Issues
- **[High] All body content JS-dependent** — invisible to non-rendering crawlers/AI.
- **[Medium] E-E-A-T depth** — no visible instructor/founder bios, DGCA approval numbers, or accreditation pages surfaced (YMYL-adjacent education niche).
- **[Medium] Duplicate content on ~25 alias routes** (e.g. /best-atpl-classes-in-india, /commercial-pilot-training-in-dwarka, /courses-and-careers) rendering the same component as canonical pages, with no canonical to consolidate.

## Recommendations
- Server-render/prerender content; add named-expert + accreditation signals; differentiate or canonicalize alias routes; add FAQ/answer blocks for citability.
