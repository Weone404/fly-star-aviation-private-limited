3. WHAT WAS FIXED (branch geo/tech-p0 — 8 commits, NOT MERGED)
================================================================================

3.1 THE 404s — 15 of 43 sitemap URLs were dead
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
Root cause: scripts/prerender.js builds its route list from
src/lib/routeMeta.ts. Any URL in sitemap.xml WITHOUT a routeMeta entry is
never prerendered, falls through vercel.json's catch-all, and returns 404.
The dead set and the "in sitemap but not in routeMeta" set matched exactly,
15 for 15.

Contributing cause: a second prerender script (prerender.mjs) that read
sitemap.xml instead, and never ran. And PRERENDER.md — the project's own
documentation — described that dead script and gave a "how to add a route"
checklist that omitted routeMeta.ts entirely. The documentation caused the bug.

Fixed:
  - Wired 2 pages that had real content: /courses/cabin-crew, /courses/ground-staff
  - Pruned 13 URLs with no page behind them from sitemap.xml
  - Removed a llms.txt entry pointing AI crawlers at a 404
  - Deleted prerender.mjs; rewrote PRERENDER.md to match reality
  - Added scripts/check-route-parity.js, wired as "prebuild" — the build now
    FAILS if this drift ever recurs

3.2 CHARACTER ENCODING — 169 broken characters across 19 files
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
UTF-8 saved as Latin-1. User-visible damage included 58 rupee signs inside
fee tables (garbled exactly where an AI engine looks for pricing), 19 emoji
on live pages, and the /rtr page's H1.

Done in two passes. The first missed 7 runs mixing two encoding types; the
second caught them, and also cleared a real pre-existing lint error.
Verified: all changed lines have identical ASCII skeletons — character
substitutions only, no structural edits.

3.3 NAV
--------------------------------------------------------------------------------
The top-level "Courses" menu item pointed at "/Courses/cpl" — capital C,
a 404 in production, on every page of the site. Now "/courses/cpl".
